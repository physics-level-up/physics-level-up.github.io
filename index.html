levelUP physics

levelUP physics publishes video lessons and computational assignments for university physics courses, including the Riemann Blue series on calculus applications in physics.

levelup-youtube uploader

The lessons are uploaded to the levelUP physics YouTube channel by a small command-line tool, the levelup-youtube uploader. The tool is used only by the channel's owner, on the owner's own computer, to upload the channel's own videos, captions, titles and descriptions. It is not offered to the public.
